# Contributing to SportDharma Ecosystem Core

Thank you for your interest in contributing to `sportdharma-ecosystem-core`. As a sovereign, FOSS (Apache 2.0), Local-First project targeting off-grid outdoor safety and communications, we maintain strict standards for code quality, architectural isolation, and compliance.

---

- All contributions are licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**.
